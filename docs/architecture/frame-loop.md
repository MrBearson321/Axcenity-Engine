# Frame order

The fixed order of one frame. Do not reorder it casually.

1. Read input. Keys for the UI's focus (Tab, the arrows, Enter, Escape while something is focused) and game controllers' buttons move focus first, through the host's `UiInteraction` (`docs/ui.md`, Keyboard and controller focus); a confirmed button fires `Activated` here, as a click does. When a `ThirdPersonController` or `FirstPersonController` in the Workspace has a body, `character` steps it here (walk or run, jump and fall, crouch, slopes and steps, and its camera: following and wheel zoom, or at the eyes) before scripts run. Only the first such controller receives the keys. Then `nav` steps every `NavigationAgent` with a body toward its `Destination`, or at its `Velocity`, and has it look and listen for the player character; hosts fire `PlayerSensed` for the senses that just started (`docs/architecture/navigation.md`).
2. Move tweens on (`TweenService`, setting their properties), then resume coroutines whose wait has expired, and fire queued events
3. Hold that character's and the agents' parts kinematic (`Controllers::keep_kinematic`, `Agents::keep_kinematic`), then step physics at a fixed 60 Hz (accumulator; may run 0–N steps per frame). Other bodies keep the same gravity and friction.
4. Fire `Touched`, then `TouchEnded` (Phase 38), then `RunService.Heartbeat`; then particles and Animators move on (Animators pose their MeshParts and fire `StateChanged`)
5. Move the loading screen on (a LocalScript's `RemoveDefaultLoadingScreen`, then `LoadingGui::advance`, which may turn its ScreenGui off), then update UI layout (`taffy`) and shape text
6. Build the draw list and render

The player runs these in `WindowEvent::RedrawRequested` (`crates/player/src/main.rs`), with a comment per step. The editor runs steps 1-4 only during Play (`Session::frame` in `crates/editor/src/play.rs`); its viewport lays out UI at step 5 using its current pixel size, then builds and draws step 6. Visual-debug overlays (colliders, raycasts, bounds, emitters, the navigation mesh, agent paths) are painted in the editor viewport after the scene texture; they do not change this order, and the player never draws them.

Since Phase 16, change events (`Changed`, `ChildAdded`, ...) for whatever changed fire at every step that runs scripts, before and after it, so physics' changes fire at the start of step 4 (see `docs/scripting/execution.md`).

Since Phase 32, `RunService.Stepped` fires on both sides between steps 2 and 3 (`ScriptRuntime::stepped`), and just before step 6 builds the frame `ScriptRuntime::render_step` runs LocalScripts' `BindToRenderStep` functions in priority order and then `RenderStepped`, before `take_camera_move` and `step_camera`, so a camera they move is drawn this frame. The player calls it right before `end_camera`; the editor's Play after the loading screen moves on and before it takes the camera move (its Animators update after it).

Since Phase 14, step 1 also hands LocalScripts the camera (`set_camera`) and the key presses since the last frame (`ScriptRuntime::input`), and step 2 first delivers RemoteEvent messages to each side (server, then client) before resuming its tasks. Where a LocalScript moved the camera (`take_camera_move`) is applied just before step 6, unless a character controller owns the view; then that move is dropped and the controller's camera is drawn. Since Phase 31, step 1 also passes the view's size (`set_viewport_size`), and just before step 6 `step_camera` moves a Follow or Track camera after its subject and moves shakes on; its `GameCamera::view` turns the host's camera into the one drawn (a CameraType other than Custom draws the script's camera instead, and the fly camera waits). See `docs/scripting/client-server.md` and `docs/scripting/api-character.md`.
