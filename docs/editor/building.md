# Build tools

Paint, materials, lights, particles, and the angular tool.

- **Tools** (modelled on F3X Building Tools): Select, Move, Resize, Rotate and Transform in the Home tab's Tools group, and Paint, Material, Anchor, Lighting, Emission and Angular in the Model tab's Build group. While editing (not typing, not during Play), single keys pick them: 1 Move, 2 Resize, 3 Rotate, 4 Transform, Escape Select (see `docs/editor/transforms.md`), and F3X's V Paint, N Material, M Anchor, U Lighting, P Emission, and R Angular. `Tool::Scale` is labelled Resize. The Collision tool is gone (removed on request): `CanCollide` is in Properties, and the Home tab's Snapping group has Collisions and Collision Groups.
- **Whole selection**: every tool acts on the selected parts and the parts inside selected Models and Folders (`viewport::selected_parts`, the last selected last). Each action is one undo step (`History::record_many`), named like "Paint 3 parts"; slider and handle drags merge until the mouse is let go. Dragging a selected part by its body moves the whole selection by the same offset (the parts being dragged are skipped when finding the surface, `scene::pick_hit_skipping`) and keeps the selection.
- **Options panel** (`build_tools.rs`): a panel at the top left of the viewport for every tool but Select, showing the last selected part's values:
  - Move: Axes **Global** (world axes, handles at the middle of the selection's world box), **Local** (handles on the last part's axes; each part moves along its own matching axis) or **Last** (all move along the last part's axes). Increment is the move snap.
  - Resize: Directions **Normal** (the dragged face moves, the opposite stays) or **Both** (both faces move, the center stays). Handles sit on the last part; each part resizes along its own axis by the same amount.
  - Rotate: Pivot **Center** (middle of the selection, world axes), **Local** (each part around its own center and axes) or **Last** (the last part's center and axes). Increment is the rotate snap.
  - Paint: 28 swatches and a color picker; picking one paints the selection, and with this tool a click on a part paints it (and the rest of the selection if it's selected) before selecting it.
  - Material (`build_tools.rs`, `app/custom_materials.rs`): three sections over the Transparency, Metalness and Roughness sliders and Casts a shadow. **Built-in** is every `Material` (picking one also clears a `MaterialVariant`, as one undo step). **Prototype** is Kenney's prototype textures (CC0, 6 color families of 13, `prototype.rs`): picking one copies its image and a material file into the project (`assets/textures/prototype`, `assets/materials/prototype`, keeping anything already there) and draws the selection in it, so the project stays self-contained. **Custom** lists the project's other material files, **New…** (name, and Albedo, Normal and Roughness images from anywhere: copied to `assets/materials/<name>/`, then applied) and **Load…** (a `.material.json` from anywhere: its images come too, found from its folder and each folder above it; its `base` is merged in; an image that can't be found is left out and reported). They're ordinary material files, so the Material tab edits them further ([assets.md](assets.md)).
  - Anchor and Collision: how many are on, and buttons to switch `Anchored` or `CanCollide` for all; Enter switches them all, as in F3X.
  - Lighting: for PointLight, SpotLight and RectLight, Add (to each selected part without one), Remove, and Color, Brightness, Range, (SpotLight) Angle and (RectLight) Width and Height for every such light in the selection.
- **Lights and emitters without a part** (as in Unreal): inserting a PointLight, SpotLight, RectLight or ParticleEmitter with no part selected puts it on its own at the camera's focus point (no Lamp or Emitter holder part). It works from its own `Position` and `Orientation` (Properties' **Placement** section), shows as an icon you can click and drag, and the Move and Rotate handles move and turn it. Put in a part, it works from the part and has no icon; taken out of one in the Explorer it stays where it was. See [../rendering/lights.md](../rendering/lights.md).
- **Reflection probes and light probes**: **Reflection Probe** and **Light Probes** on the ribbon (beside the lights, and in the Explorer's Insert menu) insert a `ReflectionProbe` or a `LightProbeVolume` into the selected part, or into a new invisible 16×10×16 `ReflectionProbeBox` or `LightProbeBox`; **Bake Probes** bakes them all into `assets/probes` as one undo step, volumes first. A volume whose bake is out of date (the world changed since) has a warning badge in the Explorer. See [../rendering/sky.md](../rendering/sky.md).
- **Atmosphere and time of day** (graphics Phase 24): **Atmosphere** on the Lighting ribbon (after Bake Probes, and in the Explorer's Insert menu) inserts an `Atmosphere` into Lighting, whatever is selected. Its Properties section **Atmosphere** has sliders for `Density` and `Offset` (0 to 1) and `Glare` and `Haze` (0 to 10); Lighting's **Time of Day** section has `ClockTime` (0 to 24 h, wrapping) and `GeographicLatitude` (-90° to 90°), and its **Fog** section `FogColor`, `FogStart` and `FogEnd` (an Atmosphere's fog replaces them). **Clouds** beside it inserts a `Clouds` into Lighting the same way; its **Clouds** section has `Cover` and `Density` sliders (0 to 1), `Speed` (studs a second) and a `Direction` slider (0° to 360°). **Weather** beside it inserts a `Weather` into Lighting too. Its **Weather** section has `Precipitation`, sliders for `Intensity`, `Wetness` and `SnowCover` (0 to 1), and `TransitionTime` (seconds). **Outlines** beside it inserts an `OutlineEffect` into Lighting (graphics Phase 31): its **Outlines** section has `Enabled` and `Thickness`, and its color is under Appearance ([../rendering/stylized.md](../rendering/stylized.md)). **Color Correction** beside that inserts a `ColorCorrectionEffect` into Lighting (graphics Phase 32): its **Color Correction** section has `Brightness`, `Contrast`, `Saturation`, `TintColor` and `Posterize`, and `LookupTable` and `Palette` rows with a folder button (`Edit::PickGradingFile`, copying a file from outside the project into `assets/grading`) and a clear button ([../rendering/post.md](../rendering/post.md)). **Graphics Volume**, **Exposure**, **Fog** and **Bloom** after them (graphics Phase 33) insert a `GraphicsVolume` (an invisible box in the Workspace) and the effects it holds; Atmosphere and Color Correction go into a selected volume instead of Lighting ([../rendering/volumes.md](../rendering/volumes.md)). **Depth of Field**, **Vignette**, **Film Grain**, **Aberration**, **Sharpen** and **Motion Blur** (graphics Phases 34 and 35, `Insertable::LensEffect`) insert a lens effect into Lighting, whatever is selected; each shows its properties in a **Lens** section ([../rendering/post.md](../rendering/post.md)). See [../rendering/weather.md](../rendering/weather.md), [../rendering/sky.md](../rendering/sky.md), [../rendering/fog.md](../rendering/fog.md) and [../rendering/clouds.md](../rendering/clouds.md).
  - Emission: `EmissiveColor` and `Emission` (0–10), and No glow.
- **Particles in the editor** (Phase 37): the viewport owns a `scene::Particles`, updated every frame from the shown world, so emitters show while editing and during Play; it keeps requesting frames while any emitter is enabled or particles are out, and is cleared at Play, Stop and on opening a project. `Emit` calls from scripts during Play (`Session::take_particle_emits`) and the Emission tool's Burst reach it as `ViewportScene::emits`. Insert ▸ Particles (toolbar and Explorer) puts a ParticleEmitter in the selected part, or on its own at the camera's focus point (it gives off particles from a 0.1-stud box at its `Position`, turned by its `Orientation`). The Emission tool adds emitters set up as each `ParticleType` (`build_tools::particle_preset`), removes them, and shows a Burst. A particle texture dropped from the Assets panel onto a part sets its emitters' `Texture`, or adds one. Properties shows an emitter's properties under Emitter and Particles; `Texture` has a Browse button that copies files from outside the project into `assets/particles`.
- **Angular tool** (Phase 36, `angular.rs`, key R), modelled on Archimedes: with a part selected, pick the face to extend from (±X, ±Y or ±Z of the part's own axes), the part's axis to bend around (not the extend axis), the angle (signed; Flip reverses it) and the number of copies (up to 360; Close loop picks `360° / angle − 1` when the angle divides a turn). Each copy is the one before slid forward by its length, then turned around the edge of its end face on the inside of the bend, so neighbours share that inner edge and a wedge gap opens outside. Orange outlines preview the copies. Extend (or Enter) duplicates the part (with what's inside it) into each place, right after it in the tree, as one undo step ("Extend Rail 23 times"), and selects the last copy so the next press carries on. Positions and orientations are tidied to 3 decimals.
