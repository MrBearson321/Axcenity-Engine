# Sky and environment

The sky behind the world, and the light and reflections it gives surfaces.

- `DrawList::sky` (`Sky`) comes from the Lighting properties `SkyTopColor`, `SkyHorizonColor`, `GroundColor`, `EnvironmentIntensity` and `SkyImage` (`scene::lighting_settings`). The sky is drawn first in the scene pass as a full-screen triangle (`fs_sky` in shader.wgsl): the gradient (`gradient_sky` in `sky.wgsl`), or the sky image, plus a sun disc and glow along the sun's direction that bloom.
- **Sky image** (graphics Phase 16). `SkyImage` is a project path to a picture of the whole sky, wrapped around the world (equirectangular: its middle toward -Z, its edges toward +Z, top straight up). A Radiance `.hdr` file keeps light brighter than white, so the sun in it lights and blooms; PNG and JPEG are read as sRGB. `Assets::sky_image` (`assets/src/sky_image.rs`) reads it once, reports a file it can't read once (Output) and drops back to the colors, and reloads it when the file changes. Reading `.hdr` uses the `image` crate's `hdr` feature (no new crate; MIT or Apache-2.0). The renderer halves an image larger than the GPU's textures until it fits and uploads it as `Rgba16Float` with every mip level. The image is shown only while the reflection quality bakes environment maps: at Low the colors are drawn and lit instead.
- **Environment lighting** comes from the sky. Diffuse light and reflections are multiplied by `EnvironmentIntensity`; the flat ambient is kept only as a small floor (0.05). Both are combined with the split-sum approximation (`ambient_specular` in lighting.wgsl), so metals reflect the sky.
- **Environment maps** (graphics Phase 16; `renderer/environment.rs`, `environment.wgsl`). Above Low reflection quality, the sky is baked when it changes (its colors, its image or the reflection quality; never every frame, and the sun and `EnvironmentIntensity` aren't baked in): drawn into a source cubemap, halved into each of its mip levels, then blurred into the reflection cubemap by GGX importance sampling, one roughness per level (0 at the top level, 1 at the 4² level), each sample reading a blurrier source level the wider it stands for (filtered importance sampling). Shading reads level `roughness × last level` (`sky_specular`). Diffuse light is the sky's second-order spherical harmonics, worked out on the CPU (`environment::harmonics`, over a 64×32 grid of the gradient or the image's 128-wide level), convolved with the cosine lobe and divided by π, and read per pixel by `sky_irradiance`. Frame bindings 10 to 12 are the reflection cubemap, its sampler and the sky image, 1×1 blanks while there are none; `FrameUniform::environment` and `sky_light` carry the rest.
- **Reflection quality** is a graphics setting ([settings.md](settings.md)): Low (the analytic path: diffuse from a ground-to-sky blend by the normal, reflections from the gradient blurred toward that blend by roughness, `environment_diffuse` and `environment_specular`), Medium (64² cubemap, 32 samples per texel), High (128², 64) and Ultra (256², 128). Low allocates nothing; `effective` lowers a cubemap larger than the GPU's textures. The maps and sky image count in `RenderStats::target_bytes`.
- The default sky looks as it did before the maps: `hello-graphics` is within about 1.6 per channel of its references at every preset (Low is exact).

## Tests

- `render/src/renderer/tests/environment_maps.rs`: metals and plastics under the default sky look as they do from the gradient (within the snapshot harness's 6 per channel at Medium, High and Ultra); a sky image shows in a mirror and behind the scene, and not at Low; a new sky bakes once however many frames are drawn, the sun and intensity don't bake, and Low frees the maps.
- `render/src/renderer/environment.rs`: the harmonics of an even sky and a sky lit from above, pixel directions matching `equirect_uv`, 16-bit floats, halving.
- `assets/src/sky_image.rs` and `scene/src/draw_list.rs`: `.hdr` and PNG sky images, cached and reported once, handed to the draw list.
