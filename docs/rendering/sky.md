# Sky and environment

The sky behind the world, and the light and reflections it gives surfaces.

- `DrawList::sky` (`Sky`) comes from the Lighting properties `SkyTopColor`, `SkyHorizonColor`, `GroundColor`, `EnvironmentIntensity` and `SkyImage` (`scene::lighting_settings`). The sky is drawn first in the scene pass as a full-screen triangle (`fs_sky` in shader.wgsl): the gradient (`gradient_sky` in `sky.wgsl`) plus a sun disc and glow along the sun's direction that bloom, or the sky image alone (it has its own sun, or none, so no disc is drawn over it).
- **Sky image** (graphics Phase 16). `SkyImage` is a project path to a picture of the whole sky, wrapped around the world (equirectangular, 2:1: its middle toward -Z, its edges toward +Z, top straight up). OpenEXR (`.exr`) and Radiance (`.hdr`) files keep light brighter than white, so the sun in them lights and blooms; PNG and JPEG are read as sRGB (8-bit ones through a 256-entry table), but nothing in them is brighter than white, so they light less and look flatter. `Assets::sky_image` (`assets/src/sky_image.rs`) reads it once, on the background texture workers when the host loads textures in the background (the editor and player do: the sky colors show until it's ready, and the "Loading textures" note counts it), reports a file it can't read once (Output) and drops back to the colors, warns once when it isn't about 2:1 (it's stretched around the sky), and reloads it when the file changes. An image wider than `MAX_SKY_WIDTH` (4096) is scaled down as it's read, in its own format, so an 8K file never takes 400 MB of linear light. Reading uses the `image` crate's `exr` and `hdr` features (`exr` brings the `exr` crate, BSD-3-Clause, with `lebe`, `zune-inflate`, `bit_field`, `half` and `pulp`; see [../project/licensing.md](../project/licensing.md)). The renderer halves an image larger than the GPU's textures until it fits (borrowing the draw list's pixels, not copying them) and uploads it as `Rgba16Float` with every mip level. The sky pass reads the level whose pixels are about the size of the screen's (from how far the direction turns between pixels, `fwidth`), blurrier toward straight up and down where the image's columns crowd together (at most 4 levels more), so detail doesn't shimmer and the poles don't break into moiré; the bake picks its levels the same way. The image is shown only while the reflection quality bakes environment maps: at Low the colors are drawn and lit instead.
- **Choosing one in the editor** (`editor/src/app/sky.rs`): Lighting's `SkyImage` row in Properties has a folder button (the system's file picker, for `.exr`, `.hdr`, PNG or JPEG; a file from outside is copied into `assets/sky`, `assets::import_sky_image`) and a button back to the sky colors, and takes a sky dragged from the Assets panel. A sky tile double-clicked or dropped in the viewport, and an `.exr` or `.hdr` imported or dropped on the window, all ask "Use *name* as the sky?" first; Use as Sky (or Enter) sets it as one undo step. See [../editor/assets.md](../editor/assets.md).
- **Environment lighting** comes from the sky, or inside a light probe volume (below) from its probes for diffuse light. The sky's diffuse light and reflections are multiplied by `EnvironmentIntensity`; the flat ambient is kept only as a small floor (0.05). Both are combined with the split-sum approximation (`ambient_specular` in lighting.wgsl), so metals reflect the sky.
- **Environment maps** (graphics Phase 16; `renderer/environment.rs`, `environment.wgsl`). Above Low reflection quality, the sky is baked when it changes (its colors, its image or the reflection quality; never every frame, and the sun and `EnvironmentIntensity` aren't baked in): drawn into a source cubemap, halved into each of its mip levels, then blurred into the reflection cubemap by GGX importance sampling, one roughness per level (0 at the top level, 1 at the 4² level), each sample reading a blurrier source level the wider it stands for (filtered importance sampling). Shading reads level `roughness × last level` (`sky_specular`). Diffuse light is the sky's second-order spherical harmonics, worked out on the CPU (`environment::harmonics`, over a 64×32 grid of the gradient or the image's 128-wide level), convolved with the cosine lobe and divided by π, and read per pixel by `sky_irradiance`. Frame bindings 10 to 12 are the reflection cubemap, its sampler and the sky image, 1×1 blanks while there are none; `FrameUniform::environment` and `sky_light` carry the rest.
- **Reflection quality** is a graphics setting ([settings.md](settings.md)): Low (the analytic path: diffuse from a ground-to-sky blend by the normal, reflections from the gradient blurred toward that blend by roughness, `environment_diffuse` and `environment_specular`), Medium (64² cubemap, 32 samples per texel), High (128², 64) and Ultra (256², 128). Low allocates nothing; `effective` lowers a cubemap larger than the GPU's textures. The maps and sky image count in `RenderStats::target_bytes`.
- **Reflection probes** (graphics Phase 17; `renderer/probes.rs`, `renderer/probe_bake.rs`, `reflected` in lighting.wgsl). A `ReflectionProbe` inside a Part reflects, in that part's box, the world as the editor baked it from the part's middle, in place of the sky. Its `Bake` names the bake file and `BlendDistance` (default 2) is how many studs inside the box's faces it fades in from what's around it. In the editor, **Reflection Probe** on the ribbon inserts one into the selected part, or into a new invisible, non-colliding 16×10×16 `ReflectionProbeBox`; **Bake Probes** bakes every probe in parts (`editor/src/app/probes.rs`): `TextureRenderer::bake_reflection_probe` draws the scene six times from the middle, a quarter turn wide along each axis, through the normal pipeline on a renderer of its own (the editor's graphics settings at 100% render scale, without UI or other reflection probes, but with light probe volumes, which are baked first) and reads the HDR scene back before post-processing, in linear light, at `PROBE_BAKE_SIZE` (128²). Each bake is saved as `assets/probes/<part name>.probe.hdr` (a probe that already has a file there keeps it), a Radiance `.hdr` holding the six cube faces stacked top to bottom (+X, -X, +Y, -Y, +Z, -Z, as a cubemap keeps them), so it's exported with the other assets and any HDR viewer opens it. The new `Bake` values are one undo step (the files stay). Bakes don't follow the world: bake again after changing it. `Assets::reflection_probe` reads a bake once (`assets/src/probe.rs`); a missing or broken one is reported once in Output and the sky is reflected there. Bakes aren't listed in the Assets panel or offered as skies. The renderer uploads each bake once, as a cubemap with every mip level, and blurs it like the sky (`Environment::filter`: the same downsample and GGX passes) into its own layer of one cubemap array at the reflection quality's size, which exists only while probes are drawn. Up to `MAX_PROBES` (8) are drawn, the nearest to the camera; the frame uniform lists them smallest box first, with each box's world-to-box matrix. A surface inside a box reads that probe where its reflection leaves the box (box-projected, so walls line up), the roughness's level; smaller boxes come first and bigger ones, then the sky times `EnvironmentIntensity`, fill what each leaves at its edges. A probe's light is the scene's own, not scaled by `EnvironmentIntensity`. Reflection probes only change reflections; diffuse light comes from the sky or a light probe volume. They're left out at Low reflection quality (below Medium), where nothing is allocated. Probes need cube array textures, which DirectX 12, Vulkan and Metal all have.
- **Light probe volumes** (graphics Phase 19; `renderer/light_probes.rs`, `bake_light_probes` in `renderer/probe_bake.rs`, `surrounding_diffuse` in lighting.wgsl, `scene/src/light_probes.rs`). Indirect light without lightmap UVs. A `LightProbeVolume` inside a Part fills that part's box with a grid of probes, about `Spacing` (default 4) studs apart along each of the box's own sides (`ceil(side / Spacing)`, 1 to `MAX_LIGHT_PROBES_PER_SIDE` = 16 a side), each in the middle of its cell (`scene::light_probe_points`). `Bake` names the bake file and `BlendDistance` (default 2) is how many studs inside the box's faces its light fades in. In the editor, **Light Probes** on the ribbon (beside Reflection Probe, and in the Explorer's Insert menu) inserts one into the selected part, or into a new invisible, non-colliding 16×10×16 `LightProbeBox`; **Bake Probes** bakes every volume, then every reflection probe. `TextureRenderer::bake_light_probes` draws each probe's six faces at `LIGHT_PROBE_BAKE_SIZE` (16²) on one renderer of its own, like a reflection probe (the editor's graphics settings at 100%, without UI, reflection probes or other volumes): the sky, and every surface lit by the sun, lamps and sky, so one bounce. The sky is drawn without the sun's disc there (`ScenePipeline::sun_disc`, `Frame::light_probes.y`), since the sun lights surfaces directly. Each probe's faces are projected on the CPU onto the first four spherical harmonics (`cube_light`: each texel weighted by the share of directions it covers, scaled to 4π), convolved with the cosine lobe and divided by π like the sky's, so a white matte surface facing `n` returns `c0·0.282095 + 0.488603·(c1 n.y + c2 n.z + c3 n.x)`. Each bake is saved as `assets/probes/<part name>.lightprobes.json` (`assets/src/light_probes.rs`: the grid's `counts`, the probes X first, then Y, then Z, four rgb coefficients each, and a `fingerprint`), kept by a volume that already has one; the new `Bake` values are one undo step. `Assets::light_probes` reads a bake once; a missing or broken one is reported once in Output and the sky lights the volume. The renderer writes the nearest `MAX_VOLUMES` (8) volumes' probes into one storage buffer (frame binding 16, four `vec4`s a probe) only when the volumes drawn change, and shrinks it to one blank probe without them; `Frame::volumes` lists them smallest box first, each with its world-to-box matrix, grid and first probe. Every surface (parts, models and terrain, still or moving) inside a box blends the eight probes around it (held at the outermost ones near the faces) and evaluates them at its normal in place of the sky's diffuse light; smaller boxes come first and bigger ones, then the sky times `EnvironmentIntensity`, fill what each leaves at its edges. A probe's light is the scene's own, not scaled by `EnvironmentIntensity`, and it still darkens with ambient occlusion. Volumes change only diffuse light, at every reflection quality. A probe inside a solid part sees through its back faces, so keep walls thicker than the gaps between probes, or move the volume's box. Lightmaps are left out unless volumes prove too coarse for interiors.
- **Real-time GI** (graphics Phase 20; `renderer/realtime_gi.rs`, `light_probe_sum.wgsl`). The machine's `realtime_gi` setting (off at every preset; `UserGameSettings.RealtimeGI`) keeps the drawn volumes' probes following the light as it changes: a moving sun, lamps, the sky. Each frame, before the scene is drawn, `PROBES_PER_FRAME` (2) probes are traced again, counting through every drawn volume's probes in buffer order (`LightProbes::point`): a `ProbeCamera` of its own (the one a bake uses, kept between frames) draws each probe's six 16² faces through the normal pipeline, one bounce as a bake sees it, with the frame's settings but at most Medium shadows; each face's HDR picture is copied into a texture array, and a compute shader (`sum_probe`, a copy of `cube_light`) sums them into the four coefficients and writes them over the probe in the light probes' buffer, so nothing is read back. A volume of n probes is retraced in n/2 frames (a 4×4×4 volume in about half a second at 60 frames a second), and a probe traced at run time matches a bake drawn with the same settings. Only baked volumes are drawn, so a volume needs a bake (its starting light) to be traced. The traced probes last while the same volumes are drawn: when the set changes, its bakes are written again and traced from there; turning the setting off frees the tracer and writes the bakes back. Its cost is a second renderer's copy of the scene's meshes and textures and 12 small scene draws a frame (each with its own shadow maps), so it's an opt-in setting; on a GPU without compute shaders it stays off with a note. Ray-traced shadows and reflections are left out: wgpu's ray queries aren't yet available on all three platforms' drivers.
- **Stale bakes.** A bake's `fingerprint` is `scene::light_bake_fingerprint` of the world it was baked from: a 64-bit FNV-1a hash (the same in every build) of every Instance in the Workspace and Lighting (class, name, place in the tree, properties, and a terrain's heights, paint and foliage), leaving out scripts and every `Bake`, which baking sets. The editor works it out again after each history change (`EditorApp::stale_light_probe_volumes`, `History::revision`) and puts a warning badge on each volume whose bake was made from a different world in the Explorer (on its closed parent too), until it's baked again or the change is undone. The fingerprint survives saving and reopening.
- The default sky looks as it did before the maps: `hello-graphics` is within about 1.6 per channel of its references at every preset (Low is exact).

## Tests

- `render/src/renderer/tests/environment_maps.rs`: metals and plastics under the default sky look as they do from the gradient (within the snapshot harness's 6 per channel at Medium, High and Ultra); a sky image shows in a mirror and behind the scene, and not at Low; no sun is drawn over a sky image; a pixel-fine checkerboard sky reads as an even grey, not noise; a new sky bakes once however many frames are drawn, the sun and intensity don't bake, and Low frees the maps.
- `render/src/renderer/environment.rs`: the harmonics of an even sky and a sky lit from above, pixel directions matching `equirect_uv`, 16-bit floats, halving.
- `render/src/renderer/tests/reflection_probes.rs`: a metal sphere in a red-walled room reflects the red walls with a probe baked there and the sky without one; each bake face sees its own wall, turned the way cubemaps keep it; a probe is filtered once however many frames are drawn, freed when it's gone, and left out at Low. `renderer/probe_bake.rs`: each face camera, mirrored, sees its cubemap face; 16-bit floats read back. `renderer/probes.rs`: box distances, the world-to-box matrix, broken bakes skipped.
- `assets/src/probe.rs` (a bake round-trips through its file; a missing or misshapen one says why), `scene/src/draw_list.rs` (a probe takes its part's box; a missing bake is reported once and the sky is reflected) and `editor/src/app/probes.rs` (a probe with no part gets an invisible box and bakes into `assets/probes`, baking again keeps the file, undo; a baked probe saves, reopens, reaches the draw list and is packed into the built game).
- `render/src/renderer/tests/light_probe_volumes.rs`: a white part moved through a lit room picks up the red or green of the wall beside it from a baked volume, looks the same anywhere lit by the sky alone, and outside the volume draws exactly as without one; baking twice gives the same light, and a probe's surface facing a wall takes its color; probes are uploaded once however many frames are drawn and their buffer shrinks without them. `renderer/probe_bake.rs`: even light all round lights every surface with its color; light from one side lights surfaces facing it. `renderer/light_probes.rs`: grids that don't match their probes, or flat boxes, aren't drawn.
- `assets/src/light_probes.rs` (a bake round-trips with every bit of its fingerprint; missing, short or broken ones say why), `scene/src/light_probes.rs` (probes fill the part's box about `Spacing` apart, at most 16 a side; a volume takes its part's box and a missing bake is reported once; the fingerprint changes with the world and lighting but not with bakes or scripts) and `editor/src/app/probes.rs` (a volume with no part gets an invisible box, bakes repeatably into `assets/probes`, is flagged after a new part and unflagged by undo or a new bake; a baked volume saves, reopens up to date, reaches the draw list and is packed into the built game).
- `assets/src/sky_image.rs` and `scene/src/draw_list.rs`: `.exr`, `.hdr` and PNG sky images, scaled down past 4096 wide, a warning for a picture that isn't 2:1, read in the background with a wake-up, cached and reported once, handed to the draw list.
