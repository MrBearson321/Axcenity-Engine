# Sprites and particles

How particle emitters become sprites.

- `DrawList::sprites` holds `Sprite { position, size, rotation_degrees, color, opacity, brightness, light_emission, texture }`: camera-facing pictures, unlit and unshadowed. `renderer/sprites.rs` and `sprite.wgsl` draw them last in the HDR scene pass, one instanced 6-corner quad each, turned to face the camera, depth-tested against solid items without writing depth, and sorted farthest first. Sprites whose turned square can't reach the camera's view are left out (`RenderStats::sprites_culled`). The color is premultiplied by opacity and the alpha written is `opacity × (1 − light_emission)` with a `One, OneMinusSrcAlpha` blend, so light emission 0 paints over what's behind and 1 adds light. Runs of the same texture are one draw call; `None` (or a texture that couldn't be uploaded) is a plain square. Brightness above 1 blooms.
- **Wind**: an emitter with `WindAffectsDrag` slows its particles toward `Workspace.GlobalWind` instead of to a stop (`v = wind + (v − wind) / (1 + Drag·dt)`), so it needs a `Drag` above 0, as in Roblox. The editor's viewport applies the wind while editing too.
- `scene::Particles` (owned by the player and by the editor's viewport, which shows emitters while editing too) turns ParticleEmitters into sprites: it gives off and moves particles each frame in world space (at most 4000 per emitter, 1000 births per frame), and `sprites` reads each emitter's look. The six built-in pictures (`ParticleType`) are drawn in code (64×64, white, shape in the alpha) and shared (`scene::built_in_particle`). A `Texture` is loaded through `Assets` like any texture; Kenney's CC0 Particle Pack images work as they are (white shapes on transparency).
