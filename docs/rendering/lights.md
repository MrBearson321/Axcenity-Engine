# Lights

Point lights, spot lights, and the editor light gizmo.

- `DrawList::lights` holds `Light { position, color, brightness, range, cone }`. `scene` builds them from PointLight and SpotLight Instances parented to a Part or MeshPart. The light sits just above the part (half the local Y size, plus 0.05 studs) so the part's outer faces are not back-facing an interior light. A SpotLight's `Direction` turns with the part. A light with `Enabled` false, or not inside a part, is skipped. A part that contains an enabled light also gets an emissive glow so the fixture itself is visible. Inserting a light with no part selected creates an anchored Neon Ball named Lamp and parents the light to it. Point and spot lights cast shadows when their `Shadows` is on (below).
- Tiled lighting: the CPU projects each light's bounding sphere onto the screen and lists it in the 32 px tiles it touches (up to 64 lights per tile, 1024 lights total; the nearest to the camera win). The shader loops over only its tile's lights. This is simpler than clustered (3D) lighting; switch to clusters if depth-heavy scenes need it.
- Falloff is smooth to zero at `range`.
- **Spot light shadows** (graphics Phase 8): a SpotLight's `Shadows` (default false, so existing worlds look the same) puts `Some(SpotShadows { near })` in its `Cone`. `near` is how far along the beam the light's own part reaches, plus 0.05 studs (0 when the beam points away from it), so the lamp a light sits on doesn't shade its own beam (`scene::shadow_near`). The shadow quality setting ([settings.md](settings.md)) picks how many spot lights cast and each one's map: Off and Low none, Medium 2 at 512², High 4 at 1024², Ultra 8 at 2048² (`ShadowQuality::spot_map`). `plan_lights` gives the nearest shadow-casting spot lights in view a layer each of one depth array (`spot_shadow_map`, allocated only while one casts, freed when none does or the quality changes its size); the others light without shadows. Each layer is drawn from the light with a perspective as wide as the cone (at most 170°, `MAX_SPOT_SHADOW_DEGREES`) from `near` out to `range`, through the sun's shadow pipeline. Casters outside the beam's bounding sphere (`cone_bounds`) or the light's view are skipped, in view of the camera or not: `lighting::casters_in` copies the rest after the scene's instances. `LightRaw::shadow` holds the layer (-1 for none) and one texel's width per stud from the light; `Frame::spot_shadows` holds the views. The shader (`spot_visibility`) pushes the point off the surface and toward the light by amounts that grow with its distance from the light, then averages four comparisons filtered between four texels like the sun's.
- **Point light shadows** (graphics Phase 18): a PointLight's `Shadows` (default false) puts `Some(PointShadows { near })` in its `Light::point_shadows`. `near` holds, for each way along each world axis (+X, -X, +Y, -Y, +Z, -Z), how far the light's own part reaches that way plus 0.05 studs (`scene::shadow_near`, as for spot lights), so the lamp doesn't shade everything below it. The shadow quality setting picks how many point lights cast and each face's size: Off and Low none, Medium 1 at 512², High 2 at 512², Ultra 4 at 1024² (`ShadowQuality::point_map`). `plan_lights` gives the nearest shadow-casting point lights in view six layers each of one depth array (`point_shadow_map`, allocated only while one casts, freed when none does or the quality changes its size); the others light without shadows. Each face is a perspective a little over 90° wide (`POINT_SHADOW_SPREAD`, so filtering at a face's edge stays inside it) from that side's `near` out to the range, drawn through the sun's shadow pipeline after the spot views, with the casters inside the light's sphere of reach and that face's view. `LightRaw::shadow` holds the light's place (its layers start at six times it) and one texel's width per stud; `Frame::point_shadows` holds the 24 views. The shader (`lamp_visibility`) picks the face along the longest axis from the light to the point, then filters it as it does a spot light's map (`lamp_shadow`).
- **Editor light gizmo** (`viewport/overlays.rs`, `paint_light_reach`): while editing, a selected light, or one inside a selected part, shows where it shines in its color. A PointLight shows three rings at its range. A SpotLight shows its cone out to `Range` (see-through sides, 8 edges, the far rim and a ring halfway), an arrow down the beam's middle, and, where that middle ray first meets another part within range (`scene::pick_hit_skipping`, skipping the light's own part), a ring the cone's width laid on that surface. View > Light Cones shows every light's reach, fainter for unselected ones. `scene::LightMarker::part` names the light's part.
