# Sound

Playing project audio from Lua.

- **Sound** (Phase 29, extended after it): put a `Sound` Instance in the Workspace (or a part), set `SoundId` to a project-relative audio file (such as `"assets/audio/chime.wav"`), then call `sound:Play()` (from the start) or `sound:Stop()`. `sound:Pause()` and `sound:Resume()` carry on from where it was. `Playing` is true while it plays: setting it to false pauses and true resumes, and a Sound saved with `Playing` true starts when the game starts, with no script. `Volume` is a non-negative linear amplitude multiplier (1 is original level, 0 is silent), `PlaybackSpeed` scales speed and pitch (1 is normal, from 0.01 to 20), and `Looped` repeats the entire file; changes to all three apply to a sound that's already playing. `Spatial`, off by default, places the sound in the world: it pans in stereo as the camera turns and gets quieter with distance, full within `RollOffMinDistance` studs (10) and silent from `RollOffMaxDistance` (150). Its place is the nearest ancestor `Part`, `MeshPart` or `Terrain`, and `Sound.Position` when it has none of those (`audio::source_position`). The editor can mark that place (View > Overlays > Emitters); the player never draws it. A non-spatial sound ignores position. `Ended` fires (on both sides) when a sound that isn't looped plays to its end, after `Playing` goes false; `Stop` doesn't fire it. Supported files are WAV, FLAC, Ogg Vorbis and MP3. Playback runs in the host, not Lua (see `GameAudio` in `audio`); stopping editor Play drops its audio and stops all playback. `examples/hello-spatial` is two bells you can fly between.

## Buses and effects (Phase 52)

Code: `crates/core/src/sound.rs` (what the world says: `SoundAction` and the mix, `audio::mix`), `crates/audio/src/effects.rs` (Kira effects, the pitch shifter and the meter), `lib.rs` (`AudioEngine`); the settings UI is `crates/script/src/audio_settings.rs`, the Mixer `crates/editor/src/mixer.rs`.

- **SoundService** is a service (`game:GetService("SoundService")`, saved in the world file as `sound_service`) that holds **SoundGroup**s. A SoundGroup has `Volume` (a multiplier like `Sound.Volume`, 1 by default) and `UserAdjustable`. A SoundGroup inside another is a sub-bus: Master with Music and SFX inside multiplies down the tree. `Sound.SoundGroup` (nil by default) routes a Sound into one; a group that's been removed from the game plays it straight out. Groups anywhere in the game work, but SoundService is where the world file and save slots keep them.
- **Effects** are children of a Sound or a SoundGroup: `ReverbSoundEffect` (`DecayTime`, `Density`, `DryLevel`, `WetLevel`), `EqualizerSoundEffect` (`LowGain`, `MidGain`, `HighGain` in dB, `MidRange` a Vector2 in hertz), `CompressorSoundEffect` (`Threshold`, `Ratio`, `Attack`, `Release`, `GainMakeup`), `EchoSoundEffect` (`Delay`, `Feedback`, `DryLevel`, `WetLevel`), `DistortionSoundEffect` (`Level`), `PitchShiftSoundEffect` (`Octave`, 0.5 to 2, same speed) and `LowPassSoundEffect` / `HighPassSoundEffect` (`Cutoff`, `Resonance`). Every one has `Enabled` (off passes the sound by) and `Priority`: higher first, equal ones in child order. They're all `IsA("SoundEffect")`, and settings are kept in range (`mix::Effect::of`). Changing a setting or `Enabled` applies while it plays; adding, removing or reordering effects or groups, or changing an echo's `Delay`, builds the group tracks again and the Sounds on them carry on from where they were.
- **How it plays**: each SoundGroup is a Kira sub-track inside its parent's (or the main track), with its effects then a level meter. Every Sound plays on a track of its own inside its group's, with its own effects; a spatial Sound's track pans and attenuates as before and also has the zone reverb last. The main track is at the master volume. Kira's effects mix equal-power, so a reverb or echo's dry and wet levels become a mix and an output level (`effects::dry_wet`); an equalizer is a low shelf, a bell and a high shelf; distortion is a soft clip with up to 24 dB of drive. Kira has no pitch shifter, so `PitchShift` reads a 50 ms window with two crossfaded heads.
- **ReverbZone** is a Part (anchored, CanCollide off, mostly see-through, 20 × 12 × 20) with `ReverbType` (`Enum.ReverbType`: Room, StoneRoom, Hallway, Cave, ConcertHall, Arena, SewerPipe; `mix::REVERB_TYPES` and `Reverb::preset`) and `FadeTime` (1 s). While the listener (the camera) is inside one (its box, or its sphere for a Ball and cylinder for a Cylinder, turned with its Orientation; the smallest wins where they overlap; `mix::zone_at`), its preset blends onto every spatial Sound over its `FadeTime`, and out over the same time on leaving. Non-spatial Sounds (music) aren't touched.
- **Master volume**: `UserGameSettings.MasterVolume` (0 to 1, default 1; LocalScripts) is the world's (`World::master_volume`), not the computer's like the graphics settings. Save slots keep it as the setting `Audio/MasterVolume`, which `SaveService:GetSetting`/`SetSetting` also reach.
- **Default audio settings UI**: when the game starts with a `UserAdjustable` SoundGroup and the Workspace has no `AudioSettings`, the engine makes one (a ScreenGui, `Enabled` false, `DisplayOrder` 10) with a panel (class `audio-settings`) of labelled sliders: "Master volume", then each adjustable group by name, in tree order. Scripts show it with `workspace.AudioSettings.Enabled = true`, from a pause menu say.
- **Volume sliders**: any Slider with an ObjectValue named `SoundGroup` (pointing at a group) or a child named `MasterVolume` inside it is a volume slider: each frame (`ScriptRuntime::step`) it runs from 0 to 1 and shows the volume; dragging it sets the volume, and a script setting the volume moves it. Give a hand-made one a small `Step` (0.01).
- **Saving**: save slots keep SoundService's contents as world state, so group volumes come back on `LoadSlot` ([../architecture/saves.md](../architecture/saves.md)). `examples/hello-mixer` has Master, Music and SFX, a cave zone, footsteps that follow the camera, and a pause menu (P) that dulls the music with a low-pass and opens the audio settings; 1 and 2 save and load.
