# ParticleEmitter

Particle emitters from Lua. Simulation lives in the host, not in Lua.

- **ParticleEmitter** (Phase 37): put one in a part (`Instance.new("ParticleEmitter")`, parent a Part or MeshPart) and it gives off `Rate` particles a second while `Enabled`, from random points inside the part, out along `Direction` (relative to the part) at `Speed` within a cone `SpreadAngle` degrees wide, living `Lifetime` seconds (±20%). `Acceleration` (world studs/s²) and `Drag` move them, and with `WindAffectsDrag` on, Drag slows them toward `workspace.GlobalWind` so the wind carries them; `Size`/`SizeEnd`, `Color`/`ColorEnd` and `Transparency`/`TransparencyEnd` blend from birth to death; `Rotation` and `RotSpeed` turn them; `LightEmission` (0 paints, 1 adds light) and `Brightness` set how they glow. `ParticleType` (`Enum.ParticleType`: `Circle`, `Spark`, `Smoke`, `Fire`, `Star`, `Magic`; also takes the name) picks a built-in picture, and `Texture` (a project image such as `"assets/particles/spark_01.png"`) replaces it. `emitter:Emit(count)` gives off a burst now (up to 1000), even while not Enabled. Particles are simulated by the host (`scene::Particles`), not Lua: they aren't Instances, aren't saved, and scripts can't read them.
- **Curves over life** (R17.1): `SizeOverLife` and `TransparencyOverLife` take a `NumberSequence`, `ColorOverLife` a `ColorSequence`; each replaces its start and end pair while set, and `nil` clears it (reading an unset one gives `nil`). `NumberSequence.new(n)` is constant, `NumberSequence.new(a, b)` goes from `a` to `b`, and `NumberSequence.new({NumberSequenceKeypoint.new(time, value, envelope), ...})` takes 2 to 20 keypoints from time 0 to 1 in order (`envelope`, default 0, is a random spread per particle). `ColorSequence.new(color)`, `ColorSequence.new(a, b)` and `ColorSequence.new({ColorSequenceKeypoint.new(time, color), ...})` work the same way. A sequence's `Keypoints` lists them; keypoints have `Time`, `Value` and (numbers) `Envelope`. Example: `emitter.SizeOverLife = NumberSequence.new({NumberSequenceKeypoint.new(0, 0), NumberSequenceKeypoint.new(0.2, 3, 0.5), NumberSequenceKeypoint.new(1, 0)})`.
- **Spawn shapes and speed** (R17.2): `Shape` (`Enum.ParticleEmitterShape.Box`, `Sphere`, `Cylinder` or `Disc`, stretched to fill the part) and `ShapeStyle` (`Enum.ParticleEmitterShapeStyle.Volume` or `Surface`) pick where particles are born; the defaults are a box volume, as before. `SpeedOverLife` takes a `NumberSequence` that scales their speed at each age (`nil` clears it; the envelope isn't used). Example: `emitter.Shape = Enum.ParticleEmitterShape.Sphere; emitter.ShapeStyle = Enum.ParticleEmitterShapeStyle.Surface; emitter.SpeedOverLife = NumberSequence.new(1, 0)`.
- **Bursts, inherited velocity and headings** (R17.2b): `Bursts` takes a `NumberSequence` whose keypoints are bursts over a loop of `Duration` seconds (time is how far through the loop, value how many particles, envelope a random ± spread; a keypoint at time 1 never fires). `VelocityInheritance` (0 to 1) adds that share of the moving part's velocity to new particles. `ShapeInOut` (`Enum.ParticleEmitterShapeInOut.Outward`, `Inward` or `InAndOut`) picks the heading: round shapes head away from their middle (or toward it, or either), and a box heads along `Direction` (`Inward` reverses it). Example: `emitter.Rate = 0; emitter.Duration = 2; emitter.Bursts = NumberSequence.new({NumberSequenceKeypoint.new(0, 30), NumberSequenceKeypoint.new(0.5, 10, 5), NumberSequenceKeypoint.new(1, 0)})`.
- **Flipbooks** (R17.2c): `FlipbookLayout` (`Enum.ParticleFlipbookLayout.None`, `Grid2x2`, `Grid4x4` or `Grid8x8`) cuts the `Texture` into frames, read left to right, then top to bottom; `FlipbookMode` (`Enum.ParticleFlipbookMode.Loop`, `OneShot`, `PingPong` or `Random`) picks each particle's frame as it ages, at `FlipbookFramerate` frames a second (OneShot spreads them over its life), and `FlipbookStartRandom` starts each on a frame of its own. The built-in `ParticleType` pictures have no frames. Example: `emitter.Texture = "assets/textures/smoke_sheet.png"; emitter.FlipbookLayout = Enum.ParticleFlipbookLayout.Grid4x4; emitter.FlipbookMode = Enum.ParticleFlipbookMode.OneShot`.
- **Collisions and sub-emitters** (R17.4): `Collides = true` makes particles land on parts with `CanCollide` on and the ground (not the emitter's own part), keeping `Bounce` (0 to 1, default 0.5) of their speed into the surface and losing `LifetimeLoss` (0 to 1) of their life each landing. A ParticleEmitter inside another with `EmitOn` set to `Enum.ParticleEmitterEmitOn.Birth`, `Death` or `Collision` is a sub-emitter: it gives off `EmitCount` particles where each of its parent's particles is born, dies or lands, and nothing by its Rate. Example: `local splash = Instance.new("ParticleEmitter"); splash.EmitOn = Enum.ParticleEmitterEmitOn.Collision; splash.EmitCount = 6; splash.Parent = rain`. An emitter with sub-emitters (or one) moves its particles on the CPU (4000 each); a big colliding emitter moves on the GPU and lands its particles only on what the camera sees.
