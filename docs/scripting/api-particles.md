# ParticleEmitter

Particle emitters from Lua. Simulation lives in the host, not in Lua.

- **ParticleEmitter** (Phase 37): put one in a part (`Instance.new("ParticleEmitter")`, parent a Part or MeshPart) and it gives off `Rate` particles a second while `Enabled`, from random points inside the part, out along `Direction` (relative to the part) at `Speed` within a cone `SpreadAngle` degrees wide, living `Lifetime` seconds (±20%). `Acceleration` (world studs/s²) and `Drag` move them, and with `WindAffectsDrag` on, Drag slows them toward `workspace.GlobalWind` so the wind carries them; `Size`/`SizeEnd`, `Color`/`ColorEnd` and `Transparency`/`TransparencyEnd` blend from birth to death; `Rotation` and `RotSpeed` turn them; `LightEmission` (0 paints, 1 adds light) and `Brightness` set how they glow. `ParticleType` (`Enum.ParticleType`: `Circle`, `Spark`, `Smoke`, `Fire`, `Star`, `Magic`; also takes the name) picks a built-in picture, and `Texture` (a project image such as `"assets/particles/spark_01.png"`) replaces it. `emitter:Emit(count)` gives off a burst now (up to 1000), even while not Enabled. Particles are simulated by the host (`scene::Particles`), not Lua: they aren't Instances, aren't saved, and scripts can't read them.
