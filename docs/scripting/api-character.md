# Third-person character

A kinematic walk, run and jump, up slopes and steps within limits. Crouch, a first-person controller and custom movement modes are later phases.

- **ThirdPersonController** sits under a `Model`, `Part`, or `MeshPart`. The body is that part, or every `Part` and `MeshPart` inside the parent. The first controller in the Workspace that has a body receives input and owns the camera. Others stay where they are.
- **Movement** is `"Walk"` or `"Run"` (either case for run). Anything else walks. `WalkSpeed` is 16 studs a second and `RunSpeed` is 32, across flat ground, relative to where the camera looks (pitch does not change the speed). Set them from Lua (`controller.Movement = "Run"`), from the set-property block, or from Properties. Shift does not change the mode; it still slows the fly camera when no controller owns the view.
- **Jump.** Space jumps during Play, and held on landing it jumps again. A script or the set-property block sets `Jump = true` to jump once; the controller sets it back to false on its next step, whether or not the character was on the ground, so a jump asked for in the air does nothing. `JumpHeight` (7.2 studs) is how high it rises, under the engine's gravity (196.2 studs/s²). A ceiling stops the rise.
- **Ground, slopes and steps.** Movement goes in slices of at most half a stud, so nothing on the way is skipped. Rays from the middle of the body, at heights above `MaxStepHeight`, treat anything that isn't walkable ground as a wall: the move stops at it and slides along it. Ground is walkable when it's no steeper than `MaxSlopeAngle` (45°, at most 89). A face lower than `MaxStepHeight` (1 stud) is a step when walkable ground lies on top of it within that height; the character rises onto it as its front reaches it. A steeper slope or a taller step stops the character; it is never launched up one. Ground is looked for under the middle and the leading edge, so the character keeps to a ledge until its middle leaves it, follows a walkable slope down, and steps down a drop no taller than `MaxStepHeight`. Off a taller drop it falls under gravity and lands on whatever solid is below. Parts with `CanCollide` off are passed through. The character is not a physics body: its parts are anchored after scripts and before physics, so a script cannot drop them for that step, and other parts keep the same gravity and friction. Its fall and jump state isn't saved; Play starts it in the air for one step, which lands it.
- **Camera.** `CameraDistance` (12, at least 1) is how far behind the character the camera sits, and `CameraHeight` (4) is how far above the feet it looks. Play starts behind the character, looking slightly down. Right-drag (the player: right button) orbits. WASD and the arrow keys move. During Play the editor fly camera is left where it was and returns on Stop. `workspace.CurrentCamera` reports this view. A LocalScript can read it; a move it writes is not applied while a controller owns the camera.
- **Insert.** Model tab, Effects, or Explorer Insert ▸ Character. If a Model, Part, or MeshPart is selected, the controller is parented there. Otherwise the editor also inserts a Model named Character and a Part named Body (2×5×1).
- **Sample.** `examples/hello-character` switches with Shift from a LocalScript and with 1 and 2 from a block script, next to a falling crate. J jumps from the LocalScript and 3 from the block script. Grey obstacles to the right (a 0.8-stud `LowStep`, and a 30° `Ramp` up to a `Platform`) are crossed; red ones to the left (a 2-stud `TallStep` and a 60° `SteepRamp`) stop the character.
