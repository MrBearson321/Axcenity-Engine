# `mesh_edit`

- Edits triangle meshes on the CPU: plane cuts that close their holes (`cut.rs`, filled by ear clipping in `fill.rs`; then `merge.rs` refills flat faces so repeated cuts don't pile up needle triangles), shapes to cut (`shapes.rs`), convex Voronoi fracture pieces (`fracture.rs`), the pieces' `.glb` writer (`glb.rs`) and the editor's Generate glue (`generate.rs`). Behavior: `docs/architecture/fracture.md`.
- Deterministic: the same mesh, count, seed and pattern give the same pieces. Never iterate a `HashMap` into output; use the built-in PRNG, not `rand`.
- Phase 55: the Model Studio's editable meshes: half-edge `EditMesh` (`half_edge.rs`), New shapes (`primitives.rs`), selection and loops (`select.rs`), a file's objects opened and saved as `.glb` (`model_doc.rs`). Phase 56: modeling operations (`ops.rs` and `ops/`; `apply`, `MeshOp`, `check_manifold`) edit a polygon `Soup` and rebuild the mesh; keep every one manifold. Phase 57: modifiers (`modifiers.rs` and `modifiers/`; `Modifier`, `evaluate`) and the `.meshedit.json` sidecar (`sidecar.rs`). Phase 58: uv unwrapping (`uv.rs` and `uv/`: LSCM on its own sparse solver, projections, packing); seams, pins and `uv2` live on `EditMesh` and go through `Soup`. Phase 59: texture painting (`paint.rs` and `paint/`: a `PaintSet` per object slot, layers kept flattened, `TexelMap` brushes, `bake_paint`; layer PNGs in the sidecar folder). Phases 60–61: sculpting (`sculpt.rs` and `sculpt/`) and baking (`bake.rs`), removed in Phase 90 and back in R15; retopology stays out (`low_poly.rs` is Make Low Poly; `bvh.rs`, `reference.rs` and `remesh.rs` serve sculpting, baking, bone heat and the Humanoid template). Phase 62: rigging (`rig.rs` and `rig/`: `Armature` to and from an `assets` `Rig` under an `Armature` root bone, the Mixamo-named Humanoid template, `Skin` weights cut to 4 continuously, bone heat and weight brushes); the Studio's Pose test must skin exactly as `assets::Posed` does (`vertex_skinning`, `skin_matrices`). Behavior: `docs/editor/model-studio.md`.
- Phase 50: solid modeling: BSP booleans of closed meshes (`csg.rs`, results closed so they combine again) and combining parts (`solid.rs`, saved as `<name>.union.glb`). Behavior: `docs/architecture/solid-modeling.md`.
- Depends on `core` and `assets` only, never `render` or `physics`. Meshes are matched by position (vertices may be split), so a cut point is computed the same way from either side of an edge.
