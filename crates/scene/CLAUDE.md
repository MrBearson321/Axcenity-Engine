# `scene`

- Shared bridge from `core::World` to `render::DrawList`, plus camera and picking. Draw-list rules: `docs/rendering/pipeline.md`.
- Keep picking consistent with rendered placement (MeshParts pick by their box), and preserve the shared orientation convention (degrees, YXZ).
- Joint constraints with `Visible` on are drawn from built-in cylinders and spheres in `constraints.rs` (coil, rope, axle).
- `Particles` is host-owned state (player, editor viewport), never stored in the world; hosts call `set_gpu` (compute shaders) and `draw`, and big emitters move on the GPU (`render::ParticleEmitter`, keep their motion matching `particles.wgsl`); see `docs/rendering/particles.md`. So are `Poses` (from `anim::Animators`), drawn by `build_draw_list_posed`.
- Depend only on `core`, `assets`, `render`, `terrain` and `ui` (UI layout into the draw list); input/window handling belongs to `player` or `editor`.
- `time_of_day.rs` places the sun and moon by `ClockTime` and `GeographicLatitude` and reads Lighting's Atmosphere (`docs/rendering/sky.md`); the defaults must keep giving the old fixed sun. `fog.rs` turns an Atmosphere or Lighting's `FogEnd` into `render::Fog` (`docs/rendering/fog.md`); the defaults must keep giving none. `clouds.rs` reads Lighting's `Clouds` (its own drift, `Speed` toward `Direction`) plus the `GlobalWind` (`docs/rendering/clouds.md`). `weather.rs` reads Lighting's `Weather`; a host's `Particles` eases it and lets its rain or snow fall (`add_weather`, `docs/rendering/weather.md`). Painted plants and parts whose material file has a `sway` get a `DrawItem::sway`, and `GlobalWind` becomes `DrawList::wind` (`docs/rendering/wind.md`). A Part whose material file has `water` draws on `render::water_surface()` (`docs/rendering/water.md`). `outlines.rs` reads Lighting's `OutlineEffect` into `DrawList::edges` (`docs/rendering/stylized.md`). `color_grading.rs` combines Lighting's `ColorCorrectionEffect`s into `DrawList::grading`, and `lens.rs` reads its first enabled lens effect of each kind (a `MotionBlurEffect` too, `docs/rendering/temporal.md`) into `DrawList::lens` (`docs/rendering/post.md`). `graphics_volumes.rs` blends the `GraphicsVolume`s around the camera into that look (`docs/rendering/volumes.md`); with none, nothing changes.
- `TerrainCache` is host-owned too: it keeps chunk meshes (same key, new version per edit) and weight textures between frames, and draws painted foliage on the surface; see `docs/terrain.md`.
