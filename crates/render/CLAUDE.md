# `render`

- Draws 3D and screen-space UI `DrawList` data with wgpu. Pipeline and materials: `docs/rendering/pipeline.md`. Other drawing topics are listed in `docs/rendering.md`; open one.
- `renderer.rs` is `Renderer` and `TextureRenderer`; `renderer/types.rs` has the GPU types and `renderer/scene.rs` draws a frame. `lighting.rs` plans uniforms, tiles, shadows and batches. `post.rs` blooms, tone-maps and runs FXAA; `tone_mapping.rs` is the CPU copy of `post.wgsl`'s curves (keep them matching). `ui_pass.rs` builds quads. `gpu.rs` uploads resources; `texture_budget.rs` shrinks the least recently drawn textures to fit the settings' texture memory budget (GPU copies of their smaller mips). `gpu_timer.rs` times each `FramePass` on the GPU for `RenderStats` (`docs/rendering/performance.md`). `graphics.rs` is the machine's `GraphicsSettings` (presets, overrides, `automatic`), `Capabilities` and `effective` (`docs/rendering/settings.md`); `TextureRenderer::limit_capabilities` forces fallbacks for tests. `occlusion.rs` draws the depth and normal prepass and ambient occlusion (`ssao.wgsl`) into `FrameTargets`, which owns every frame target. `environment.rs` bakes the sky into environment maps (`environment.wgsl`) only when it changes, and works out its spherical harmonics on the CPU (`docs/rendering/sky.md`); `probes.rs` filters reflection probes' bakes into a cubemap array (`ProbeRaw` matches `Probe` in lighting.wgsl) and `probe_bake.rs` bakes one (`TextureRenderer::bake_reflection_probe`) or a grid of light probes (`bake_light_probes`); `light_probes.rs` keeps light probe volumes' probes in one storage buffer (`VolumeRaw` matches `Volume` in lighting.wgsl), and `realtime_gi.rs` traces a few of them again each frame with a kept `ProbeCamera`, summing them on the GPU (`light_probe_sum.wgsl`, a copy of `cube_light`: keep them matching); `fog.rs` packs `DrawList::fog` (`FogRaw` matches `Fog` in fog.wgsl, which every scene shader and sprite.wgsl include) and runs volumetric fog's froxel grid (`volumetric_fog.wgsl`, compute, seeing the frame's bindings through a compute-visible copy of `frame_layout_entries`; `docs/rendering/fog.md`); `clouds.rs` packs `DrawList::clouds` (`CloudsRaw` matches `Clouds` in clouds.wgsl, which every shader including lighting.wgsl includes) and keeps the wind's drift between frames (`docs/rendering/clouds.md`); `DrawList::weather` is `Frame::weather`, which `light_surface` applies first (`weathered`, `docs/rendering/weather.md`); `water.rs` is `Water` and the wave grid (`water_surface`); `renderer/water.rs` draws water first among see-through items and, from Medium reflection quality, splits the scene pass to copy the solid scene (`water_copy.wgsl`) into `SceneCopy`, bound in frame binding 7's place (`water_frame_bind_group`), for `water.wgsl` (`docs/rendering/water.md`); `DrawList::wind` is `Frame::clock.yzw` and `DrawItem::sway` is `InstanceRaw::sway`, which `swayed` in shader.wgsl applies in `vs_main` and `vs_shadow` alike (`docs/rendering/wind.md`); `Material::shading` is `MapsUniform::shading` and `toon`, and `fs_main` picks `light_surface`, unlit or `toon_surface` (a toon ramp is bound in the specular map's place; `docs/rendering/stylized.md`); `Material::outline` is `MapsUniform::outline` and `Batch::outline`, drawn after the solid items by `outline_pipeline` (`vs_outline`/`fs_outline`, the standard shader whatever the surface shader), and `DrawList::edges` keeps the prepass alive (`targets_used`) for `edges.wgsl`, drawn after the sky through `OcclusionTargets::edge_group`; the sky pass is drawn after solid items, kept by its depth test to uncovered pixels. `atmosphere.rs` is the CPU model of air scattering (`Atmosphere`, its transmittance table `Air`), and `renderer/atmosphere_sky.rs` turns a `Sky` into what's drawn (`SkyLook`: colors dimmed by daylight, or an atmosphere's sky image, worked out only when it changes). `sky.wgsl` is shared by the scene and bake shaders, and its gradient has a CPU copy there. `surface_shaders.rs` checks a material's WGSL `surface` function with naga, reports its errors on its own lines, and caches its pipelines by the code's hash (`SurfaceShader`; the code goes after `gpu::SCENE_TEMPLATE`, in surface.wgsl's place: shader.wgsl's `SurfaceInput` and `Surface` are its API, `docs/rendering/models.md`). `lod.rs` picks each item's level of detail with hysteresis and cross-fades (`LodStates::resolve`, before upload) and applies `DrawItem::cull_distance`. `culling.rs` tests bounding spheres against each view's `Frustum` (`plan_draws` culls the camera's, `casters_in` each shadow view's); a caller's mesh is bounded by its corners (`GpuMesh::bounds`), never assume the unit cube for one. `sprites.rs` draws particles. `fonts.rs` lists the built-in UI fonts (`BUILT_IN_FONTS`, plus bold and italic `VARIANTS`); a project's fonts come in `DrawList::fonts`. `renderer/text.rs` picks each rich text span's face, fits `TextScaled` text and makes outline, shadow, faux bold and decoration passes (one shaped copy per paint layer).
- Do not add dependencies on `core`, Instances, scripts or winit; world-to-draw conversion belongs in `scene`. Animated meshes are skinned on the GPU (`skinning.rs`: `MeshSource::skin_joints`/`skin_weights`, `DrawItem::skin`, `InstanceRaw::skin`); meshes whose corners change otherwise keep their key and change `MeshSource::version`, and `update_custom_mesh` rewrites them. The shadow pipeline's instance layout gives each attribute's offset by hand: keep them matching `InstanceRaw`.
- Keep `SpriteRaw` and `SpriteUniform` (`renderer/sprites.rs`) aligned with `sprite.wgsl`, and `UiVertex` (`UI_BOX`, `UI_SHADOW`, `UI_INSET_SHADOW`, its `clip` box, `NO_CLIP` for none) with `ui.wgsl`; text is cut to a `UiClip` through glyphon's bounds. Turned UI items (`rotation_degrees`) turn their quads; glyphon can't turn text, so `renderer/turned_text.rs` draws turned text upright onto a sheet (premultiplied, read by `fs_sheet`) and draws that turned with the images. `ui_pass.rs` cuts sliced images (`UiSlice`) into pieces.
- Keep `InstanceRaw`, `FrameUniform` (lighting.wgsl's `Frame`), `MapsUniform`, the vertex layout and material bindings aligned with `shader.wgsl`, and coordinate wgpu API changes with the workspace version. The item shader reads 16 sampled textures (8 frame, 8 material), wgpu's default per-stage limit: a new frame texture must share or replace a binding. Its vertex and instance layouts use all 16 vertex attributes (the default limit): pack a new per-item value into an existing one.
