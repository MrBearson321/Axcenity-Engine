# `render`

- Draws 3D and screen-space UI `DrawList` data with wgpu. Pipeline and materials: `docs/rendering/pipeline.md`. Other drawing topics are listed in `docs/rendering.md`; open one.
- `renderer.rs` is `Renderer` and `TextureRenderer`; `renderer/types.rs` has the GPU types and `renderer/scene.rs` draws a frame. `lighting.rs` plans uniforms, tiles, shadows and batches. `post.rs` blooms, tone-maps and runs FXAA; `tone_mapping.rs` is the CPU copy of `post.wgsl`'s curves (keep them matching). `ui_pass.rs` builds quads. `gpu.rs` uploads resources; `texture_budget.rs` shrinks the least recently drawn textures to fit the settings' texture memory budget (GPU copies of their smaller mips). `gpu_timer.rs` times each `FramePass` on the GPU for `RenderStats` (`docs/rendering/performance.md`). `graphics.rs` is the machine's `GraphicsSettings` (presets, overrides, `automatic`), `Capabilities` and `effective` (`docs/rendering/settings.md`); `TextureRenderer::limit_capabilities` forces fallbacks for tests. `occlusion.rs` draws the depth and normal prepass and ambient occlusion (`ssao.wgsl`) into `FrameTargets`, which owns every frame target. `lod.rs` picks each item's level of detail with hysteresis and cross-fades (`LodStates::resolve`, before upload) and applies `DrawItem::cull_distance`. `culling.rs` tests bounding spheres against each view's `Frustum` (`plan_draws` culls the camera's, `casters_in` each shadow view's); a caller's mesh is bounded by its corners (`GpuMesh::bounds`), never assume the unit cube for one. `sprites.rs` draws particles. `fonts.rs` lists the built-in UI fonts (`BUILT_IN_FONTS`, plus bold and italic `VARIANTS`); a project's fonts come in `DrawList::fonts`. `renderer/text.rs` picks each rich text span's face, fits `TextScaled` text and makes outline, shadow, faux bold and decoration passes (one shaped copy per paint layer).
- Do not add dependencies on `core`, Instances, scripts or winit; world-to-draw conversion belongs in `scene`. Animated meshes are skinned on the GPU (`skinning.rs`: `MeshSource::skin_joints`/`skin_weights`, `DrawItem::skin`, `InstanceRaw::skin`); meshes whose corners change otherwise keep their key and change `MeshSource::version`, and `update_custom_mesh` rewrites them. The shadow pipeline's instance layout gives each attribute's offset by hand: keep them matching `InstanceRaw`.
- Keep `SpriteRaw` and `SpriteUniform` (`renderer/sprites.rs`) aligned with `sprite.wgsl`, and `UiVertex` (`UI_BOX`, `UI_SHADOW`, `UI_INSET_SHADOW`, its `clip` box, `NO_CLIP` for none) with `ui.wgsl`; text is cut to a `UiClip` through glyphon's bounds. Turned UI items (`rotation_degrees`) turn their quads; glyphon can't turn text, so `renderer/turned_text.rs` draws turned text upright onto a sheet (premultiplied, read by `fs_sheet`) and draws that turned with the images. `ui_pass.rs` cuts sliced images (`UiSlice`) into pieces.
- Keep `InstanceRaw`, `FrameUniform`, `MapsUniform`, the vertex layout and material bindings aligned with `shader.wgsl`, and coordinate wgpu API changes with the workspace version.
