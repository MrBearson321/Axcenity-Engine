# `core`

- Owns the Instance tree, generational arena, typed properties (including UI `UDim2`) and events, `TerrainData` (heights, paint, foliage) and the world's `StringTable`, active language and game `UiSettings` (`ui_settings.rs`, saved in slots as `UI/…` settings); no engine-crate dependencies.
- Boundaries: `docs/architecture/crates.md`. Instances: `docs/architecture/data-model.md`. Inspect `src/world.rs` and `src/class.rs` before changing the tree or schema.
- `World::copy_from` copies a subtree from another world (levels load this way), leaving scripts out. `prefab.rs` (Phase 49): a placed copy's `PrefabLink`, its overrides (children matched by name and occurrence) and `sync`; no file access (that's `project::Prefabs`).
- Network IDs (Phase 64): a replica (`World::empty_replica`) keeps its server's Instances at their own IDs (`create_at`) and its own above 2³¹ (`InstanceId::is_local`); ServerStorage and ServerScriptService are `server_only_services`.
- Mutate Instances through `World` so links and change records stay consistent. Property defaults/types also drive project serialization; apply `Material` before dependent properties.
- `tool.rs` (Phase 45): where a Tool is (`carried`: in hand or put away in an Inventory), which physics, scene, character, ui and script all ask. `csg.rs` (Phase 50): parts inside a UnionOperation are out of the world (`out_of_world` covers both). `dialogue.rs` (Phase 77): `DialogueView` and `QuestView`, what the dialogue box and quest tracker show. `sound.rs`: `SoundAction` and the mix the world describes (SoundGroups, effects, ReverbZones; `audio` re-exports it as `mix` and plays it). `ability.rs` (Phase 76): `AbilitySlot`, what the ability bar shows (`script` makes it, `ui` draws it). `input.rs`: the input map (`input.json`'s actions), `InputEvent`s and the held `InputState` every host and the script runtime share. `streaming.rs` (Phase 80): what loads together (`Partition`, `Unit`), used by `net` and `scene`; `Instance::revision` and `World::structure_version` let readers skip what hasn't changed (`docs/architecture/streaming.md`); `revision_without_motion` and `motion_revision` split off a BasePart's `MOTION_PROPERTIES` (roadmap M7).
- `spline.rs` (Phase 73): a Spline's curve through its SplinePoints (`SplineCurve`), shared by script, nav, anim and net (`docs/architecture/splines.md`). Phase 74: a SplineMesh's or SplineScatter's children are generated (`is_generator`, `generator_of`); anything that saves, sends or snapshots the tree must leave them out.
- `ThirdPersonController` and `FirstPersonController` are declared here (shared properties: `movement_properties`). The `character` crate steps it; do not make `core` depend on `character`.
